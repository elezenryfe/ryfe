Before we build

Two traps I found, and the open questions

Both of these were checked against live documentation rather than memory, and both would have shown up as mysterious bugs weeks in.

Google would log you out every 7 days

While an OAuth app sits in Testing, Google expires its refresh tokens after a week. The app works, then one morning every calendar call fails with invalid_grant and it looks like a bug in Ryfe. The fix is to publish the app to In production and simply not submit it for verification — Google's personal-use exemption covers you under 100 users. But do not upload a logo, because that alone puts you in the review queue.

DeepSeek retires model names fast

deepseek-chat and deepseek-reasoner were retired in July 2026. Today it is deepseek-flash and deepseek-v4-pro — and your own stock_evaluation project is still calling the older deepseek-v4-flash, which now only works as a temporary alias. So Ryfe reads the model list from the API at startup instead of hardcoding anything.

The Google client you have won't work

The GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID in your .env belongs to your jobtailor and finance_app sign-in flows — those are Web clients, and a web client can only use a redirect URI it registered in advance, port included. Calendar needs a Desktop client, which is a two-minute job in Cloud Console. It's step one, and nothing else waits on it.

Thinking mode is quietly on

DeepSeek defaults to thinking, and in that mode temperature is silently ignored, tool_choice: "required" returns a 400, and the reasoning text has to be echoed back on every turn or the next call fails. Ryfe turns thinking off for normal chat and keeps it behind a "think harder" toggle. Your existing DeepSeek code already does exactly this.
